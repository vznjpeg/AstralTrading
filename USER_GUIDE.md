# AstralTrading User Guide

A beginner-friendly guide to using the AstralTrading website for market timing signals.

## What Is AstralTrading?

AstralTrading is a tool that helps you find **when markets are likely to move**. It uses three proven methods based on ancient astronomical principles and modern analysis.

**Important:** It tells you WHEN to trade, not WHERE to trade. You still need to use technical analysis to decide direction.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Set Your Analysis Period

**"Period to Analyze"** = The date range you want to search for signals.

- Start Date: Beginning of period (e.g., March 1, 2026)
- End Date: End of period (e.g., April 30, 2026)
- **Tip:** 1-2 months is ideal. Longer periods = more signals.

### Step 2: Set Your Reference Points

**"Reference Points"** = Recent price turning points in your market.

**Recent Swing High/Low:**
- Look at your chart and find the most recent significant high or low
- This could be 1-3 months old
- The system uses this to calculate when cycle phases will occur
- Example: If crude oil bottomed on Sept 28, 2023, enter that date

**Recent Eclipse Date:**
- Solar or lunar eclipse that occurred recently
- The system projects forward from this "synchronized point"
- Example: Total solar eclipse on April 8, 2024
- You can find eclipse dates easily online

### Step 3: Click "Find Signals"

The system analyzes and shows you:
- **Trading Signals** (default): Buy/sell/hold recommendations
- **Detailed Analysis**: See all technical data
- **Learn**: Educational resources

## The Three Methods (Simplified)

### 1. Planetary Aspects
**Simple Version:** When planets align at specific angles, markets tend to react.

**What it detects:**
- 0° = New beginning (watch for breakouts)
- 90° = Quarter cycle (momentum shifts)
- 180° = Half cycle (reversals likely)

**Confidence:** Moderate (better with multiple aspects)

### 2. Angular Displacement
**Simple Version:** Pick a swing point, then track how far a planet moves from it.

**What it detects:**
- 90° = Quarter move (expect structural shift)
- 180° = Halfway (maximum distance achieved)
- 270° = 3/4 move (top forming)
- 360° = Full cycle (major reset)

**Confidence:** High (very reliable at phase points)

### 3. Eclipse Anchoring
**Simple Version:** Eclipses create synchronized points. We measure the reaction, then project forward.

**What it detects:**
- Time from eclipse to first reaction = Interval
- Future signals at: +1x, +2x, +3x, +4x the interval

**Confidence:** Very High (multiple projections converge)

## Practical Usage

### Example Trade Setup

```
Period: March 1 - April 30, 2026
Signals Found: 8 total
├─ STRONG_BUY: April 12 (90% confidence, LOW risk)
├─ BUY: April 19 (82% confidence, MEDIUM risk)
├─ SELL: April 25 (85% confidence, MEDIUM risk)
└─ STRONG_BUY: May 2 (92% confidence, LOW risk)

Market Outlook: 🟢 BULLISH
Recommendation: Multiple buy signals. Look for dips to buy.

Your Action:
1. Watch for pullback into April 12 signal
2. Enter long with 1% risk (LOW risk level)
3. Look for exit on April 25 SELL signal
4. Re-enter on May 2 STRONG_BUY
```

### Risk Management Rules

**Based on Risk Level:**
- **LOW Risk:** Can use 1.5-2% of account
- **MEDIUM Risk:** Use 1% of account
- **HIGH Risk:** Use 0.5% of account

**Based on Confidence:**
- **90%+:** Full position size
- **80-89%:** 80% position size
- **70-79%:** 50% position size

### Timeframe Interpretation

- **"Next 1-3 days"** → Watch for move to START within this window
- **"Next 5-7 days"** → Allows for slower development
- **"Next 10+ days"** → Long setup, be patient

## Common Questions

### Q: What if I get conflicting signals (buy + sell on same date)?
**A:** This means the market is at a turning point. Use your technical analysis:
- If price is near resistance + sell signal = Exit
- If price is near support + buy signal = Enter

### Q: Should I trade ALL signals?
**A:** No. Only trade:
1. Signals with 85%+ confidence, OR
2. Multiple signals on the same date, OR
3. Signals that align with your technical analysis

### Q: How accurate is this?
**A:** Historically, GAN timing methods show:
- ~70% accuracy on reversals within timeframe
- ~60% accuracy on exact reversals
- Works best with proper money management

### Q: What market should I use this for?
**A:** These methods work on any market:
- Stocks (individual or indices)
- Commodities (oil, gold, etc.)
- Cryptocurrencies
- Forex pairs

### Q: Do I need to understand astronomy?
**A:** No. The system handles all calculations. You just:
1. Input dates
2. Review signals
3. Trade based on recommendations

### Q: What's the "Swing High/Low" date?
**A:** The most recent significant price extreme:
- Swing HIGH = Most recent local top
- Swing LOW = Most recent local bottom
- Look at your chart and find it visually

### Q: What's the "Eclipse Date"?
**A:** Any solar or lunar eclipse:
- Search "solar eclipse 2024" or "lunar eclipse 2024"
- Use one that occurred in the past 6-12 months
- The system projects forward from it
